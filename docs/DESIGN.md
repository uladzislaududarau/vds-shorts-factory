# Studio design

The original design used Sites locally and the design-taste-frontend app-screen workflow: True-black flat, seed 905817144, without a redraw. The visual foundation is a dark editing surface, lighter surface levels, an inverted primary action and color reserved for data and progress. Inter is bundled locally, including Cyrillic glyphs. The product motif moves from a wide video frame to a vertical crop and then through a five-stage pipeline.

Each screen answers a practical question: Dashboard shows what is ready and what to start; Sources identifies the material to process; Analysis explains which moment to choose; Clip Studio previews the intended file; Library shows downloadable outputs; Queue identifies stages needing attention; Settings explains processing location and resource use.

Do not invent customers, results, data or metrics. E2E fixtures are explicitly labeled as authored QA material and use synthetic macOS speech. Responsive layouts are checked at 1440, 768 and 390 pixels: the sidebar collapses, forms reflow and queue tables become rows. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
